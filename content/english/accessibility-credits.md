## Who buys credits

**Development teams** use them inside their sprint rhythm: a review, an answer, and on with the
work.

**Product owners** ask about findings, priorities, and how to explain a decision to a board.

**Agencies** get a sparring partner who looks over their projects without buying a separate audit
for every client.

**Clients who already have a report** use credits for questions, re-checks and sprint testing
after the audit is delivered.

## Where the answers come from

Your question goes to a senior auditor who knows your site or your report. Since 2019 we have run
more than 950 audits, as of 7 August 2026, and that is what the answers are built on. You get
explanation, code examples and context, pitched at your background: developers get code, product
owners get plain language.

## Questions about your own report

Until August 2026 those were free. That changed. Our reports are written to be used without a
briefing: every finding opens with a user story from a visitor with a disability, and carries a
screenshot, a code example and a fix. Anything you ask us on top of that is work, and work has to
be paid for somewhere. Clients whose report was already in progress when this changed keep their
questions free until that project is finished.
