faqs:
  - question: "Do you report in English?"
    answer: "Yes. We write every report in English or Dutch at no extra cost, and our team works with clients in the Netherlands, Sweden and Germany. Findings, user stories and code examples are all in the language your developers use, so an international team can start fixing without translating anything first."
  - question: "What does an audit cost?"
    answer: "It depends on the number of unique page types and the amount of interaction, not on the number of pages. A simple website starts at around €2,250, most websites land around €3,150 and a complex application around €5,100. An app costs €2,150 per platform. Prices exclude 21% VAT."
  - question: "Do you retest after we have fixed the issues?"
    answer: "Yes. After your fixes we recheck every earlier finding on the same sample: is the problem gone, is it fixed correctly, and did the fix create a new issue. You get an updated report with a status per finding, which you can use as evidence in your accessibility statement or towards your regulator."
  - question: "Which standard do you test against?"
    answer: "We test against WCAG 2.2 level AA. The legal benchmark under the European Accessibility Act is the European standard EN 301 549, whose version 3.2.1 points to WCAG 2.1 AA. WCAG 2.2 is backwards compatible, so a WCAG 2.2 audit covers the 2.1 criteria plus the new ones at level A and AA."
  - question: "Do you audit mobile apps?"
    answer: "Yes. We test iOS and Android apps by hand on real iPhones, iPads and Android devices, with VoiceOver and TalkBack, against WCAG 2.1 in combination with EN 301 549. You get the same report per component as for a website, with a user story, a screenshot and a fix for every finding."
  - question: "Are you independent?"
    answer: "Yes. We do not build websites, do not manage content and never audit our own work. Our own tool, the WCAG Radar, is a checker your team runs in the browser; it does not change your site. No overlays, no conflict of interest: what you buy from us is a report your team can fix from."
  - question: "Does the EAA apply to us?"
    answer: "The European Accessibility Act covers services offered to consumers, such as webshops, banking, telecom and passenger transport. Microenterprises (fewer than 10 employees and at most €2 million turnover or balance sheet) are exempt for services, not for products. In the Netherlands the ACM, AFM and ILT enforce it. Your lawyer gives the legal answer; we show where your website stands."
