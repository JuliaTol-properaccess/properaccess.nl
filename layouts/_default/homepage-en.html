Self-assessment is too big a risk

The European Accessibility Act has applied since June 2025. For many webshops and online services that means your product is already non-compliant today. The reaction we hear most: “we will send someone on a course and judge it ourselves.” That sounds efficient, but the risk is too big.

And that risk is concrete: complaints to the regulator (opens in a new window), claims from advocacy organisations, reputational damage — and a site your visitors still cannot use.

gavel Duty to report

What the law asks of you

  • You must report to the regulator if you do not meet the accessibility requirements.
  • The only exception: proving it would be “a disproportionate burden or a fundamental alteration”.
  • Supervision: the regulator checks compliance and confirms every report.
