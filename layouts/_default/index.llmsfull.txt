Independent audit firm for digital accessibility. Since 2019 we have carried out more than 950 audits: 236 assignments for 63 clients, among them the Rijksmuseum, the provinces of North and South Holland, NRC, the Dutch Museum Association, Plus and Jumbo. 40% of our clients come back for a second assignment. Those figures are current as of 7 August 2026.

What sets us apart:
- **We teach you how to test it yourself**: a report tells you what is wrong today, we make sure you spot it yourself next time
- **A report organised per element**, not per WCAG success criterion the way the rest of the market does it
- **Visual examples** and language pitched at whoever has to act on the finding
- **Independent**: we do not build or maintain websites, so we never audit our own work
- **Direct contact with a senior auditor**: for clients who have had a report from us; before that a relationship manager is your point of contact
- **Our own research**: we study weekly and test how browsers and assistive software actually behave
- **Our own tools, entirely EU**: built on what we found in our audits, made and hosted in Europe
