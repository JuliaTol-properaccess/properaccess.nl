How many companies in the Netherlands publish accessibility information?

Every Monday our EAA monitor checks the footer of Dutch websites in seven sectors for a link to an accessibility statement. A statement is a promise, not proof: whether a site works for someone with a disability only shows in a test of the site itself. But a company without one has not started.
